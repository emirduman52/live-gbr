The boutique

LiVe GbR sits in the centre of Erding. It is a boutique in the original sense: a single space with a deliberate edit.

Everything in the store is Italian women's fashion. Not a broad category with a few Italian pieces — Italian fashion, full stop. Coats, blouses, trousers, leather bags, and accessories, each chosen for quality and for how they work together.

The experience is personal. There is no algorithm suggesting what you might like. There is the collection, and there is a fitting room.
